# The Director's answers on temple and community dogs, 8 October 2026

A transcription of Part 1, item 1 of the sheet *"What we are waiting on"*
(Lanna Care, 8 October 2026), which the Director answered by hand and Lutan
scanned as `Answers for what we need 081026.pdf` (Lutan's Desktop, `LCA`).

The scan is not committed: its second page names a person, and this repository
is public. Only the part the community-dogs work rests on is transcribed here.
Her handwriting is given exactly, spelling included.
