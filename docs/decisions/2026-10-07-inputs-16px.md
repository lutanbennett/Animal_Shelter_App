# Inputs and selects are 16 px below md (2026-10-07, `inputs-16px`)

**Decision.** One rule in `src/app/globals.css`, below 768 px: `textarea`, `select` and every
`input` that takes typed text get `font-size: 16px`. Checkboxes, radios, files, ranges, colours,
buttons and hidden inputs are excluded — Safari does not zoom for them.

**Unlayered on purpose.** The backlog item said "base-layer rule". In Tailwind v4 utilities sit
above `@layer base`, so a field carrying `text-sm` (most of them) would keep 14 px and the rule
would do nothing. The rule is therefore outside any `@layer`, which beats every layer. That is
also why no form file needed editing.

**What was and was not measured.**
- Before the rule, the extended check (`scripts/check-phone-width.mjs`) found fields under 16 px
  (e.g. the `/residents` search box, `q`). After it, none on the pages run.
- The sideways scroll itself did **not** reproduce in headless Chromium, before or after: Chromium
  does not zoom on focus. The check focuses every field and measures width, but it can only prove
  the *cause* is gone (no field under 16 px), not watch an iPhone zoom. Same as `notes-box-phone`.
  So the 2026-10-07 Notes-box fault was only **partly** fixed by that item: textareas were fixed,
  inputs and selects were not, and could have caused the same fault on other fields. Whether
  tapping now stays put is for Lutan to confirm on the iPhone.

**What the check asserts now.** For every page view, in every focused-field pass: no visible
`textarea`, `select` or typed-text `input` in `main` computes under 16 px, and the page does not
overflow sideways once each is focused. It does not look at fields outside `main`, or at hidden or collapsed fields (width 0).
