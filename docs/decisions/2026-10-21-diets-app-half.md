# Diets app half: what reads recipes now, and what still reads `diet_types`

Follows `2026-10-21-a-diet-is-a-recipe-of-ingredients.md` (schema `0239`).

- **Settings → Diets** has two parts. *Diets* are recipes: a card per diet with one line per ingredient (small / medium / large amount, and a "measured in" choice of the ingredient's own unit or any unit in its conversions, so "1 cup" becomes kg through `item_unit_conversions`). *Ingredients* is the old list, minus the per-size amounts and the Standard flag, which nothing reads any more. Standard is now `diets.is_standard`, moved with two statements (clear, then set) because supabase-js cannot wrap them in a transaction; if the second fails the shelter has no Standard (the page says so), never two.
- **A resident's diet** stores `diet_id` and `portion_factor` (blank = full recipe). The "this looks like a typo" warning is now more than 8 meals or a portion above 3.
- **Read through recipes:** the hub's Diet section and card, the enclosure special-diet markers, the archive PDF, the residents export, stock usage (a resident on a diet is "on" every ingredient in it), and the forecasts (already, via `0239`). Purchasing and the cashflow needed no change: the forecast functions return one row per ingredient.
- **The 0239 bridge is fought, not used.** Its trigger gives every new `diet_types` row a same-named one-ingredient diet, which would put "Chicken" in the diet picker. `createDietType` removes that diet again if it is left empty and unused. The cleanup migration should drop the bridge.
- **Not done, and why (needs a schema PR; this stream adds no migration):** `special_diet_list` (Feed Special Diets and its diet sheet, `0140`/`0166`) still joins `resident_diets.diet_type_id` to `diet_types` and filters on `diet_types.is_standard`; `record_intake` and the intake form still take a `diet_type_id`. Until they read recipes, the diet form writes the legacy `diet_type_id` when an ingredient carries the diet's own name (every diet that existed before recipes), so those surfaces behave as today; a diet created since has none and stays off the sheet and out of intake. **Do not release this app half without that follow-up schema PR.**
- **Proof of the headline number:** `scripts/check-diets-app.mjs` splits dev's Standard into kibble 1/4 and chicken and rice 3/8 each in a rolled-back transaction: kibble need falls to exactly a quarter, chicken and rice appear in `diet_forecast` and `stock_diet_forecast`, and the three together equal the old total.
