Nothing was fixed and no card was proposed; every finding is a row in `docs/card-access-faults.md`. Money's 7 cards are `card-review-1-b-money`'s.

The job was done end to end where it could be: open, fill, save, reload, confirm the record landed. `docs/card-walkthrough.md` was used for its step lists only, not its verdicts.

## What carries over

- **Two cards do nothing, and the page they name does not ask for them.** `operations_landing` and `management_landing` give no cell, so alone they cannot even enter the app, and `/operations` and `/management` open for anyone whose cells open one tile in the section, card or no card (`requireAnyPageIn`). Card review 2 has to decide what these two are for.
- **`assistant` has the same shape as `view_residents`'s accident in reverse**: a card that depends on another card's read to do anything. The reads it needs are `resident.record` and `facility.enclosures`, not a card of its own.
- **The who-and-where finding from the first half reaches here too.** `do_maintenance` reads and can export the whole resident record because `resident.record` read is the record since 0259; the picker of every login's email is the same family (surfaces `0259` left without a card).
- **The stock jobs are sound.** All three hold exactly one yes/no cell and do their advertised job; what is wrong around them is menu entries and links that point at pages the card does not grant. That closes the two stock findings in `docs/backlog.md` (offered things the card does not grant, and the chain the card-only login cannot follow) into the fault log.
