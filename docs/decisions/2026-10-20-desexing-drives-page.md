# 2026-10-20 — Desexing drives: the page half (`desexing-drives-page`)

The schema (`0233`) merged as #778; this is the app on top of it. No migration.

- **Public page `/services/desexing`**, read live (`force-dynamic`) from `public_desexing_drives` only. Each drive shows the date and time, venue, species taken, and the cost and eligibility text *only when the shelter typed one*; places left; and the sign-up form while `accepting_signups`. A cancelled drive is shown as cancelled with no form; a full one says so. Nothing on the page, in the manual or in the dictionaries is a date, price or rule: they are all data from Management.
- **Services menu.** The header group now holds one link (Desexing drives), so it shows; the footer's Help column gains the same link. It is shown whether or not a drive exists: the page says none is scheduled. Hiding the entry when there are no drives was considered and dropped, because a menu that appears and disappears is harder to find than a page that says "check back soon".
- **`/services` is not edge-cached** (`NOT_EDGE_CACHED_PAGE_PREFIXES`), for the same reason as `/appeals`: a copy kept for ten minutes would offer a full or cancelled drive. The form posts to a server action, not the edge.
- **The sign-up is written as the anon role, always.** The action builds a plain `supabase-js` client from the anon key instead of `createClient()`, which would carry a signed-in staff member's session and run as `authenticated`. A staff member trying the form therefore meets exactly the limits a visitor does. The insert asks for nothing back (no `.select()`), so nothing a person typed is echoed and no sign-up is ever read from the public site. The database's refusals (`desexing_drive_full`, `_not_open`, `_species_not_taken`, `desexing_signup_too_many_for_contact`, `_rate_limited`) are mapped to plain sentences.
- **Rate limiting is the database's, as `0233` decided.** The schema note said the page half "should add a per-IP limit at the Worker if it wants one, and a honeypot field". This PR adds neither: a Worker limit is a second mechanism that needs the real-IP header and a store, and a honeypot is cheap but only stops naive bots; the accepted abuse (a patient person can fill a drive; staff remove the rows) is unchanged. Revisit if junk sign-ups appear.
- **Management → Desexing drives** (`/management/desexing-drives`, tile and route-registry entry; guard `website.content`, the cell the tables' RLS asks): list with sign-up counts, a drive form (starts blank, status Draft), and per drive the sign-up list with a status picker and Remove. Deleting a drive cascades to its sign-ups, so it asks first. The sign-up list is rendered only here.
- **Drives are not on the home page.** The view could serve a strip; not built, since there are no drives yet. A follow-up if the shelter wants it.
- **Thai.** The page's fixed words and the Management screen are in both dictionaries. What the shelter types into a drive (title, cost, who can come) is shown as typed in both languages; these fields are not on the translation queue.
