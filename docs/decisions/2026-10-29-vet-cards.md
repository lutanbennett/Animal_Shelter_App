# Four vet cards (0281)

The nine activities a vet performs were carried by no card sized for them, and `reference.add_while_recording` by no card at all
(`CLAUDE.md`: *"No card for the activity? Create one."*). Nothing here was a decision for Lutan. Not an outage: no login holds the Doctor
role (Lutan, 2026-10-25), so this is a prerequisite for the first vet's card set.

|---|---|---|---|
| `record_vet_care` | `medical.visits`, `procedures`, `blood_tests`, `prescriptions`, `immunizations`, `diet` at edit | `view_medical` | `/residents` |

| `adoption_news` | `resident.adoption_news` at edit | `view_residents` | `/residents` |
| `add_while_recording` | `reference.add_while_recording` | `view_medical` | `/residents` |

## Why new cards and not the existing ones
`medical_records` restores six of the nine but also grants `medical.archive`, `visit.book` and `placement.hospital`; `residents` restores two
but grants move, rehome, register and edit of the record. A vet should not get either bundle to recover those cells.

## The design question that was the stream's: `reference.add_while_recording`
**Its own card.** The cell is asked by the medication, frequency and procedure-type insert policies (`0148`), which anyone recording may
need, not only a vet. Folding it into `record_vet_care` would force that card onto anyone who needs to add a missing medicine. It includes
`view_medical` so a holder reaches the forms it is used in. It grants a real database insert right, so it is not a card that does nothing.
It is **not** a hard-coded-security mechanism and is not in the registry.
