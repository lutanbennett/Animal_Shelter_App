### Payments — decided 2026-11-13 (option 1: manual for both)

- **Thai buyers:** PromptPay into the shelter's account.
- **Overseas buyers:** a Wise bank transfer or a Wise payment link. Staff confirm the money has
  arrived and mark the order paid.
- **No card processor for now.** Wise is not a card gateway, and card acceptance is closed to new
  Wise Business customers (wise.com/gb/blog/how-to-take-payments-online). A card processor later
  would break the zero-ongoing-cost rule through per-sale fees, so it is **a separate, later
  decision**, not part of the shop's first build.

### Supplier orders

Each supplier has its **own delivery method**, set on the supplier screen. Today the only method is
**one email a day listing every order part not yet sent to that supplier**; parts are marked sent,
so nothing repeats and nothing is missed. Other methods (a file, a link, a supplier's own system)
are **added later as further options, without changing the shop.**
