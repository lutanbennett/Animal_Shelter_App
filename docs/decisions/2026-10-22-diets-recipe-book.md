# Settings → Diets is a recipe book: four screens, no data change

Lutan, 2026-10-21: the Diets page was "really difficult to use and manage properly", with diets and ingredients on the one page. Screens only; `0239` and `0242` stay as built.

- **Four routes instead of one page.** `/admin/diets` (a card per diet), `/admin/diets/[id]` (that diet's recipe), `/admin/diets/ingredients` (the list) and `/admin/diets/ingredients/[id]` (one ingredient). A two-button tab row (`DietTabs`) switches between the first and third. Phone first; the old "Best on a larger screen" notice is gone from these pages because the Director opens them on a phone.
- **One reader, `src/lib/diets/recipe-book.ts`.** All four pages load the same five tables once (`diet_types`, `diets`, `diet_ingredients`, `resident_diets`, `item_unit_conversions`) and filter in memory; at shelter scale that is cheaper to reason about than four queries per page.
- **"Residents on it" counts today's residents, not records.** The card says how many residents are on the diet now (a current `resident_diets` row: started, not ended, distinct residents). The delete rule still counts *every* record ever written, because a diet that was ever recorded is part of a resident's history; the recipe page says "Can't delete: N resident records use it" so the two numbers are never confused.
- **The total daily serve groups by what a line is measured in.** 1 cup + 1.5 cups + 1.5 cups reads "4 cups"; a recipe mixing cups and grams reads "1 cup + 100 g". Amounts in different units are never added together, because there is no honest conversion for a cup of chicken to a cup of oats' worth of grams.
- **Measures live on the ingredient's page, not a separate panel.** `ItemUnits` (the per-item part of `UnitsPanel`) is exported and takes `plain`, which swaps "Base unit" and "How many kg in one" for "1 cup weighs about 120 g" and "About how many g is one". Medications still use the original wording. The server actions are unchanged (`management/units/actions.ts`).
- **The two rules are enforced where they always were**, in `recipe-actions.ts` (`deleteRecipe`, `removeRecipeLine`) and `actions.ts` (`deleteDietType`); the screens add the disabled button and the sentence. Create actions now return the new row's id so "Add a diet" and "Add an ingredient" land on the new page.
- **Not changed, on purpose:** prices and stock stay on Management → Diet stock (a link on the ingredient page); Purchasing and its libraries were not touched; no migration. Other screens that might want the same treatment (medications) are out of scope and were not touched.
