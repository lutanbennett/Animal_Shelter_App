## Purpose

Meant to put a **Management** tile on a person's home screen, a doorway to the manager's pages. It gives no access of its own: it carries no permissions at all, so on its own it opens nothing.

## Gives access to

### Pages

- **Held on its own, nothing.** A login holding only this card is sent to the **public website** from every staff address tried: Home, Management, the Dashboard and the Medication list. It has no menu and no tile.
- **Held with another card, one extra home tile.** With Dashboard added, the home screen showed two tiles, Dashboard and **Management**. The Management page lists only what the holder's other cards open (here, the Dashboard), the same as for Dashboard alone.

### Tables

- None.

### Forms

- None.

## Major restrictions

- **It does nothing alone.** A person given only this card is signed in and looking at the public website.
- **It is not what opens Management.** Dashboard alone already showed a Management entry in the left menu and a Management page holding one tile. The card changes only whether a home-screen tile is drawn.
- **It does not list locked doors.** The page showed only pages the holder's other cards open. It is not the Enclosures problem (a page that looks right and is wrong); it is a tile that is harmless.
- **Nobody holds it**, and it has no grants, so the coverage check is silent about it.

## Recommendation

- **Decide (yours): should this be a card at all?** My advice: no, for the same reasons as Operations. It grants nothing, the page and menu entry open without it, and all it controls is one tile. Proposing or removing a card is Card review 2; nothing was changed.
- **If it stays:** describe it plainly as a home-screen tile, not as access to Management.
