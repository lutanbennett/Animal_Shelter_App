## Purpose

Runs the public website's own content: the big photo on the welcome page, the Pet of the week, the headline figures, the wording of each page in English and Thai, how to reach the shelter, the
Donate page, the photo strip in "Our story", and which pages are shown at all. Everything on this page is public, and the page says "Changes go live immediately."

## Gives access to

### Pages

- **Website** (the card's own page), in six tabs: Home page (hero photo, Pet of the week, Impact figures), Contact & settings, Pages (show or hide a page or a whole section), Donate, Gallery, Projects.
- **Hidden pages**: a hidden page still opens for this login so it can be checked, with a banner saying it is hidden.
- **The same Residents list and Assistant page as any signed-in login**: the Residents page opens (not counted here); the Assistant page opens and says the account cannot use it.

### Tables

- **The website's content** (wording, photos, figures, contact details, which pages are hidden): read and change.
- **Public residents** (names only, for the Pet of the week): read.
- **Gallery photos**: add, move, remove.

### Forms

- **Pages: Hide / Show a page**: works, with **one click and no confirmation**. International adoption was hidden and a visitor then got "Not found" for it (the test system showed the
  change at once; the page says the live site can take up to ten minutes because it keeps a copy). Put back with Show.
- **Pet of the week, Change a resident**: works. The list offered the residents who are public (two on the test system). Picking one showed "Featured resident updated." and
  that resident's name was on the public Home page straight away. Put back.
- **Gallery, Add photo**: works. The photo was added to the end of the strip with no description, and **the picture itself could be opened by anyone with no sign-in immediately**. The Home page shows only
  the first three photos, so a fourth does not appear until it is moved up. Removed again, after an "Are you sure?" box.
- **Hero photo, Impact figures, wording and contact details** were looked at but not changed. The impact-figures save is described as asking first.

## Major restrictions

- **This card is the public site, and it changes it in one click.** Hiding a page, switching the Pet of the week, adding a photo to the public strip: each took one press, with no
  second person and no preview before it went live. A section row has its own Hide button (not pressed). Of the cards that can change what the public sees, this one asks for the least before it does.
- **A photo added here is public the moment it is saved**, to anyone who has its link, even before the page shows it. Photos filed through Add Shelter Photos wait for approval; photos added
  here do not.
- **A hidden page leaves a trail.** The Adopt page still carries a link in its text, "How international adoption works →", to the page that was hidden; a visitor following it meets "Not found".
- **It cannot choose a resident who is not public**: the Pet of the week list shows only those already on the website. That is a good limit, and the page says so.
- **Cannot open** the Memes page, Add photos, Approve photos, Run appeals, Outreach, Stocktake or the Medication list (all refused).

## Recommendation

- **Add a check before the page goes live:** for Hide, and for Pet of the week, show what will change (which menu entries and links go) and ask once, as the Impact figures already do.
- **Decrease or add a second card:** consider a separate card for the photo strip and the hero photo. A person who only changes the opening hours should not be able to add a public photo.
- **Fix:** an added gallery photo has no description (alt text), which the public page needs for accessibility; ask for one when adding.
- **Nothing to increase.**
