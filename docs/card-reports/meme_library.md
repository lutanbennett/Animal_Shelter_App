## Purpose

Keeps the shelter's social-media memes in one place: browse them, add one, record where and when each was posted (Facebook, Instagram, LINE, TikTok, other), and archive one that
should not be used again. The page says the memes are "Never shown on the website".

## Gives access to

### Pages

- **Meme library** (the card's own page): the memes with a search box and filters (Resident, Tag, Shared on, Photo from), and the buttons "Make a meme" and "Add a meme".
  Opened with 4 memes.
- **A meme's page**: the picture, Download, Record a share, Edit, Archive, where it has been shared, its caption, the residents tagged in it and its tags.
- **Add a meme**: choose a picture, a title, captions in English and Thai, "Choose residents", tags.
- **Make a meme**: "Residents" (choose a resident, then one of their photos) and "Upload a photo".

### Tables

- **Memes and where each was shared**: read, add, change, record a share, archive.

### Forms

- **Add a meme**: works. A picture and a title were enough; it saved and showed the new meme's page.
- **Record a share**: works. "Facebook · 26 Oct 2026" appeared under "Where it has been shared".
- **Choose residents** (tag the animals in a meme): **does not work.** The list says "No residents match."
- **Make a meme, from a resident's photos**: **does not work.** Searching for a real resident ("Kame") answered "No resident matches that." Only "Upload a photo" can be used.
- Edit and Archive were not pressed.

## Major restrictions

- **Memes stay private.** Driven: the new meme's picture came back 404 to a visitor with no sign-in and did not appear on the Home, Adopt, About, Our work, Friends or Appeals pages. The
  page's promise is true.
- **It cannot tag the animals in a meme, or build one from a resident's photos**, because the card cannot read residents. The page that lists a resident's memes ("Each resident's page lists the memes
  they are in") will therefore never list one tagged by this login.
- **Cannot open** the other cards' pages: the Website page, Add photos, Approve photos, Run appeals, Outreach and the Medication list were all refused. The Residents page opens from the menu (and the
  Assistant page opens, saying "Your account can't use the assistant").
- **The page offers a resident filter and a "Photo from" filter** that this card cannot fill in.

## Recommendation

- **Increase (needed, small):** give the card the resident look-up, so that "Choose residents" and "Make a meme" work. A card for memes that cannot choose an animal does half its job.
- **Nothing to decrease:** the card reaches nothing the public can see, and it is the narrowest of the social cards.
