## Purpose

Lets a person write one short note after each visit to a temple or village where the shelter helps dogs without taking them in: where, what was done
(fed, treated, sterilised, vaccinated, rehomed), how many dogs, and optional photos.

## Gives access to

### Pages

- **Outreach visits**: the list of visits, with a running count of dogs helped (this counts help given, not different dogs).
- **Record a visit**: three steps (date and place, what we did, how many dogs).
- **Edit visit**: the same details, with Delete this visit.

### Tables

- **Community outings** (read, add, change, delete).
- **Outing places** (temples and villages: read, add).
- **Outing photos** (add, with a visit).

### Forms

- **Record a visit**: works end to end. A new place was added in step one, "Fed" ticked, 2 dogs and a note entered and a photo attached; the visit came
  top of the list and the total went from 3 to 5 dogs. The photo row was saved.
- **Edit visit** opens with every detail filled in.
- **Delete this visit** is offered. Not pressed.

## Major restrictions

- **Needs no resident and reads none**, so nothing here is held back by the resident-read fault the money cards suffer from.
- **Photos cannot be added or removed after saving.** The edit page has no photo control; photos can only be attached when a visit is first recorded.
- **The photos stay inside the app.** The page says none is shown on the website unless someone ticks it. Where that is ticked, and by which card, was
  not looked at here.
- **Menu offers Residents although the card grants nothing there.** Already in the fault log.
- **Cannot open** the medication list: "You don't have access".

## Recommendation

- **Keep as it is.** One narrow job, and it works completely. Offering it to volunteers who go on the visits is reasonable.
- **Check later:** which card ticks an outreach photo for the public site, since this is a route from a card to the public website.
