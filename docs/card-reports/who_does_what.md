## Purpose

Lets a person open Settings → Who does what and see which cards every role holds, and what each card gives. The card is described as read only: giving and taking away cards stays with Admin. It is the card that shows how the whole system is wired, so the person holding it sees everyone's access.

## Gives access to

### Pages

- **Who does what.** It opens. It lists **every role** (34 on the test system, including the throw-away roles made for this review and the Doctor), and for each one shows the cards it holds, the cards still available, the feature groups and the left-hand menu setting. Tapping a card shows exactly what it gives and what it includes.
- **Who does what: Details**, the full grid of every activity against every role, with Download CSV and Print. It opens and shows every role's cells, including the Doctor's.
- **The Management menu entry**, which opens a page holding one tile, Who does what.

### Tables

- **Roles, the cards each holds, and each role's permission cells**, read only. The page reads them for the whole system, not only for the holder's own role.

### Forms

- None. Every box on the page (names, features, the menu switch) is greyed out, and there is no Add, Remove, Move or Save button anywhere. The page says "Only an Admin can give or remove cards."

## Major restrictions

- **It cannot change anything.** No control is offered, so no role's cards could be changed with this card alone. (No save was attempted: the page offers nothing to press, and a save on a real role is not part of this review.)
- **The card's grant says "edit" but the card does read only.** The login held one permission cell, "see roles", at the **edit** level, not read. Nothing on the page uses that level, so nothing goes wrong today, but a card described as read only that holds an edit cell is a trap for the day another page asks for the same cell.
- **It shows everything about everyone's access.** The whole picture of who can do what in the shelter, every role by name, is open to the holder, and a CSV of it can be downloaded. That is deliberate for Management, but anyone given this card can map the shelter's security. It is a wide read for one card.
- **Cannot open** Settings (the Admin landing), the Security page, Operations or the Medication list (all refused).
- **The Residents entry is drawn in the menu** although this card holds nothing about residents.

## Recommendation

- **Keep it read only, and say so in the grant:** give the card a read cell instead of edit so the card and its description agree. It is the single most revealing card in the system, so it is right that it cannot hand out cards.
- **Decide (yours):** is "see every role and every cell, with a download" what you want any holder to have? If it is meant for Management only, that is a role decision, not a card one; if it should be given more widely, consider a narrower card that shows a role's own cards only.
- **Decrease (small):** hide the Residents menu entry.
