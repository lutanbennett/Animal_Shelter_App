**Second draft, 3 October 2026, with Lutan's answers.** Almost every cell is now
an answer he gave. Six cells still carry a **?**: a guess that needs a yes or a
correction. Nothing has been built.

**You, at your desk at night (Admin): everything.** That is not a column,
because nothing is ever taken away from Admin. The six columns are everyone
else, and you by day on your phone.

- **Edit** — can do it, or change it.
- **Read** — can see it, and cannot change it. For a list or a report that
  nobody changes, Read is the most there is.
- **None** — cannot see it or do it.
- **?** — not said yet. This is our guess. Please confirm or correct it.
- **↓** — less than this person can do in the system today.
- **who and where** — sees only who the resident is and where it lives. No
  background, no history, no medical records.
- **own clinic's** — a vet sees and changes only what belongs to the residents
  their own clinic treats.

## What changes from today

- **Three new roles**: the 2IC, the Head of Maintenance and the Head of Medical.
  Each can do its own job and nothing else. Today the system has no such roles,
  so whoever does those jobs signs in as Management or Staff, which can do far
  more than the job needs.
- **Only you change anything about a resident.** Intake, moving, hospital,
  foster, adoption, a death, vet visits, medical records, photos: all yours, as
  Management by day or Admin at night.
- **Volunteers see who a resident is and where it lives, and nothing else.**
  Today a volunteer can read every medical record, add and remove photos, move a
  resident between enclosures, count the stock, add photos to maintenance jobs
  and projects, mark their own recurring tasks done, ask the assistant, and see
  contacts' names and phone numbers. All of that goes (the **↓** cells). The map
  is not built yet; until it is, the enclosures list is their map.
- **A medication list is new.** It shows the Head of Medical who gets what and
  how much. Nothing is recorded.
- **Vets are on hold.** A vet's login stays exactly as it is today.
- **Everyone but you at your desk works on a phone**, you included by day. So
  every page in the six columns has to work on a phone.
