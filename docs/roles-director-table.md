# Who can do what at Lanna Care: a draft for the Director

**Draft, 3 October 2026. Nothing here is decided.** It is Lutan's notes from
talking to you and from his whiteboard, turned into one table, so that you can
correct it. Every cell with a **?** is a guess that needs your answer.

**You, at your desk at night (Admin): everything.** That is not a column,
because nothing is ever taken away from Admin. The six columns are everyone
else, and you by day on your phone.

- **Edit** — can do it, or change it.
- **Read** — can see it, and cannot change it. For a list or a report that
  nobody changes, Read is the most there is.
- **None** — cannot see it or do it.
- **?** — you did not say. This is our guess. Please confirm or correct it.
- **↓** — less than this person can do in the system today.
- **NEW** — not built yet.
- **own clinic's** — a vet sees and changes only what belongs to the residents
  their own clinic treats.

## What changes from today

- **Three new roles**: the 2IC, the Head of Maintenance and the Head of Medical.
  Today the system has no such roles, so whoever does those jobs signs in as
  Management or Staff, which can do more than the job needs.
- **Volunteers become read-only.** Today a volunteer can add and remove photos,
  move a resident between enclosures, count the stock, add photos to
  maintenance jobs and projects, mark their own recurring jobs done, ask the
  assistant, and see contacts' names and phone numbers. All of that goes (the
  **↓** cells). They keep the residents and the enclosures, to look at. The map
  is not built yet; until it is, the enclosures list is their map.
- **Giving medication is new.** Today the system records what was prescribed and
  nothing about what was actually given.
- **Everyone but you at your desk works on a phone**, you included by day. So
  every page in the six columns has to work on a phone.

## Eight questions for you

1. **Is there anyone at the shelter who is not in this table**: other
   employees, kennel hands? If not, who moves a resident, sends one to hospital,
   records a foster, an adoption or a death: only you, or the 2IC and the two
   Heads as well? (rows 5, 6, 7)
2. **By day on your phone** you have recurring tasks, vet appointments, intake,
   and residents' details and medical records. Which of these do you also want
   on the phone, and which only at your desk: contacts, the clinic list, Shelter
   Friends, the dashboard, cashflow, translations, projects? And does the 2IC do
   anything besides the stocktake, ordering, deliveries and maintenance tasks?
3. **The Head of Maintenance**: do they change anything about the residents
   (move one, add a photo), or only look? (rows 1, 5, 15)
4. **The Head of Medical** gives the medication. Do they also: write or change a
   prescription; book a vet visit; record weight, vaccinations and diet; count
   the medicine stock; add a medicine that is missing from the list? And is
   medication given at fixed times of day (a morning round, an evening round)?
   (rows 9 to 14, 23, 34)
5. **Volunteers**: when a volunteer opens a resident's page, do they see the
   medical records, or only who the resident is and where they live? (rows 10 to
   13)
6. **Vets**: should a vet be able to see whether the medicine they prescribed
   was actually given? (row 12)
7. **Vet visits and maintenance**: who books a vet visit: only you, or the 2IC
   and the Head of Medical as well? And does the 2IC only work through
   maintenance tasks, or also create and assign them? (rows 9, 17)
8. **Photos on the public website**: today, anyone who adds a photo to a
   resident who is on the Adopt page puts it on the website, unless they file it
   as Medical. Who should be allowed to do that? (row 15)
