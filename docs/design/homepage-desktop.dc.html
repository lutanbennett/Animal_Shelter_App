Our story

Lanna Care for Animals began as a grassroots response to the dogs and cats living rough on the streets of Chiang Mai — sick, injured, or with nowhere else to go.

Every animal who comes through our gate gets the same start: a vet check, a safe place to recover, and a carer who knows their story. Some move on to foster or adoptive homes. Many others stay with us for the rest of their lives.

Beyond the shelter, we run sterilisation drives and care for community dogs at temples and villages nearby.
