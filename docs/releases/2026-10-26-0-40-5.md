**What shipped.** Eight notes, the most in a while, and seven of the eight are about cards. The manual
now opens on the topics for the cards you hold rather than on your role's name (#950); a resident's
hub shows only the parts of the record your cards let you read, instead of showing a card you cannot
open and saying "0 · none" (#954); and release notes are themselves now tagged **by card** rather
than by role (#956), so what you are shown no longer depends on what your shelter calls its roles.
`0265` is the schema half of card review 1 (#960): recording a death becomes its own card, four
medical cards drop from the whole resident record to who-and-where, Do maintenance stops listing
residents, Residents photos wait for approval, and deleting a delivery becomes Admin only.

**This release mailed nobody, by decision and by construction.** `major: false`. Lutan was asked
whether it should email him, with the words "major" and "minor" deliberately absent
(`docs/release-procedure.md` step 4) — **the fourth release running where that rule has held**. Both
deploys confirmed it themselves: each printed `no major release new to <env> (was 0.40.4), so no
email.`

**It cost two touches, which is the runbook's stated target: one answer and one command.** The thing
that did it was the same as `0.40.4`'s — **folding every foreseeable question into one round**. Three
decisions were put to him before the cut, not as each arose: the mail, `0265`'s narrowing of what
five cards may read, and the thirteen test plans still awaiting a person. He chose no mail, go ahead,
and ship the thirteen as a stated gap.

**`main` moved during the release, and the shipping tree did not.** #961 (a planner handover) merged
between the cut and the merge. The code-identity diff against the tested SHA `9db37279`, restricted to
`src/`, `worker/`, `package.json`, the lockfile, `supabase/` and `next.config.ts`, is **empty** — so
what shipped is byte-identical to what was tested, and #961 is docs and a skill file. **Recorded as a
measurement, not as the "main never moved" property, which is retired.**

## The deploys

  started, allowed to finish, and **confirmed from `wrangler deployments list`** before the production
  command was handed over. **The Pi builds are what parallelised** — they build on the Pi and touch
  nothing in the main checkout
- [x] **One deliberate deviation from the runbook, kept from `0.40.4` because it earned its place:**
  the test Pi build ran first and was verified off `/api/version` and its own asset check before the
  production Pi build was started. Production's Pi is what users see, and one extra build cycle is

  main checkout at the same time
