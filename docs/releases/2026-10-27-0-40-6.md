**What shipped.** Seven notes, six of them tagged by card. The headline is that two cards which never
worked now do: **Add Shelter Photos** answered "Not authorized" on every upload (`0269` item 1) and
**Approve / Wrong animal** failed with a row-level-security refusal (`0269` item 2). Both were silent
failures in the sense that matters — the button was there and did nothing useful. Beyond that,
`0268` splits Medical records and Contacts each into a view card and a manage card, splits Publish
Shelter Friends out of Contacts, and gives nine cards the reads they need to do their own job;
`0267` puts Record weight and Feed special diets jobs into My tasks and stops a Medical-folder photo
becoming a resident's public profile picture.

**This release mailed nobody, by decision and by construction.** `major: false`. Lutan opened the
request with the word **"minor"** — which is exactly the ambiguity `docs/release-procedure.md` step 4
exists for, since in his usage it means the middle number and in this repo `major` means "worth an
email". He was asked the email question with both words absent and answered **no email**, so the
version moved the last number. **Fifth release running that the rule has held.** Both deploys
confirmed it themselves: each printed `no major release new to <env> (was 0.40.5), so no email.`

**It cost two touches — one answer and one command — which is the target**, plus one pause he asked
for himself (an urgent Cloudflare upgrade on the Pi), which is not an avoidable cost and is not
counted as a third. **The thing that did it was the same as the last two releases: folding every
foreseeable question into ONE round** before the cut — the mail, the seven unsigned plans, and the
Send to hospital consequence. Three releases running.
