## What shipped, in the four notes a user sees

1. **Recording a donation has a Donor wants a tax deduction tick**, with the donor's 13-digit
   ID, the person-or-company choice, and a *Gifts to record in e-Donation* list under
   Management → Donations. The ID is kept seven years, is visible only to logins that issue
   receipts, and is not printed on the receipt
2. **The public Shelter Friends page loads its little maps only as you scroll to them**, so the
   page opens quicker on a phone. The *Open in Google Maps* link is there straight away
3. **A sold-out product on the merchandise shop shows a Sold out badge** over a dimmed photo
   with a struck-through price, and a sold-out line in the cart is marked as soon as the page
   opens, with *Place order* waiting until it is removed
4. **The merchandise catalogue marks a product Sold out on the shop** beside its name (tagged
   `run_merch_shop`). A product with a supplier is never sold out, however low the shelf stock

Three are untagged, so everyone sees them; the fourth is for the shop's keeper. **That was
measured rather than assumed** — the cut's test plan probed `/releases` with a login holding
exactly `run_merch_shop` (all four present) and one holding no shop card (the fourth absent,
the other three present), with no `[object Object]` and no `undefined<` on either.

**The other 23 PRs shipped no user-visible change**, and that was answered per plan rather than
inferred from an empty `unreleased`: the multi-tenancy programme's part 2 and the donation
tax-ID schema (nine migrations with no reader yet), the Worker's host-to-shelter header, a shop
order-flood bound, developer checks, planner documents and `0.47.0`'s own record.
