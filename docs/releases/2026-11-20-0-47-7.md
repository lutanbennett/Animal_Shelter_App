## Why it was cut, and why it stopped here

**It was cut to unblock UAT, not because a release was due.** `#uatsb` left UAT refusing **every**
sign-in: `wrangler.jsonc` set `SUPABASE_URL` for `test` and `production` and not for `uat`, so
`worker/shelter.mjs` never looked a host up, no `x-shelter-id` reached the app, and since `0392` no
shelter header means no rows — **so every person was told their own account had no access.** #1765 fixed
the setting; a Worker deploy was needed to make it real; and **`deploy.mjs` ships only a written-down
release**, so #1766's note had to move into an entry first.

**Then it was superseded within the hour.** #1774 merged a release note of its own between this cut and
the production deploy, so the production deploy refused — correctly — and Lutan chose to cut `0.47.8`
rather than hold the icon back. **Nothing about `0.47.7` was wrong; it was simply overtaken.**
