**What shipped.** Six notes. The theme is that buttons now follow the login's cards rather than showing
and then refusing: photo Remove / Set as profile / Move to folder, Attach files and Remove file on
blood tests and procedures, Book a vet visit, and the return-from-hospital and return-from-carer links
on a resident's edit page. Beside that, Settings → Website → Pages gains a live preview of unsaved
edits in English and Thai; the manual's last fourteen topics are linked to cards, so Settings topics
such as Security show only to Admin; Settings → Who does what gains a Delete this role button that
refuses while a live login holds the role; and Settings → Security is redesigned as one row per person
with role chips.

**Under it, four migrations finish the role rebuild.** `0270` deletes the archived `staff` role
outright; `0271` adds the Translations card and its grants in the same file; `0272` takes role
archiving out entirely, rewriting eight security-definer functions and dropping a trigger; `0273`
drops two dead clinic-login policies and the function only they called.

**This release mailed nobody, by decision and by construction.** `major: false`. Lutan was asked the
email question with the words "major" and "minor" absent, as step 4 requires, and answered **no
email**, so the version moved the last number. **Sixth release running that the rule has held.**

**It cost three touches, not two, and the extra one was not the release's fault.** One answer and one
command is the target and was the plan, and the single question round held — the mail, the thirteen
unsigned plans and the quiet-failure risk were folded into one round before the cut, **fourth release
running**. Cloudflare's API then timed out from this machine for several minutes, so the production
Worker deploy had to be run a second time. Defect 1.

## What the migrations actually did to production

The before/after diff of `role-cells` and `role-cards`, which is the only record of what was taken
away:

| Role | Cells before → after | Cards before → after |
|---|---|---|
| doctor | 2 → **2** | 1 → 1 |
| head_of_maintenance | 8 → **8** | 4 → 4 |
| head_of_medical | 8 → **8** | 7 → 7 |
| management | 39 → **39** | 18 → 18 |
| second_in_command | 23 → **23** | 11 → 11 |
| volunteer | 4 → **4** | 3 → 3 |
| ~~staff~~ (archived) | 31 → **row deleted** | 13 → **row deleted** |

**No live role lost or gained anything.** Deleting `staff` took nothing from anybody, because the row
was archived and no live login could hold it — which was read from production before the apply rather
than inferred from the migration header. That is the whole of `0270`'s effect on who can do what.

**Two cards are now held by no role at all: Manage medical (`medical_records`) and Assistant
(`assistant`).** `staff` was their only holder, so this is a bookkeeping change rather than a loss —
but it means **Send to hospital is still held by nobody**, carried unchanged from `0.40.6`. It is a
card assignment in Settings → Who does what, and it is row 1 of what is left for Lutan.

**`0272` removed role archiving entirely**, which closes a reporting fault rather than creating one:
the read-only checks used to list archived roles as though they were live, and that is what led
`0269` to reason about a role nobody could hold. With no `roles.archived_at` and no archived rows,
the fault cannot recur.

  endpoint or a database read. The six card behaviours this release changes — above all whether the
  right buttons now appear for the right logins — are in the manual list below and **have not been
  seen by a person**
- **The hidden-button changes fail quietly by design**, which is the class of change Lutan normally
  holds a release for. It was put to him separately from the unsigned-plan count, with the option of
  deploying test first and looking; he chose to deploy both and look afterwards

- **The Thai wording** of the new Pages preview has not been read by anyone who reads Thai
