### During the deploy

**The deploy took three attempts.** The first two failed at the wrangler upload
step, both with the same thing:

```
▲ [WARNING] A fetch request failed, likely due to a connectivity issue.
X [ERROR] fetch failed
```

The third succeeded. Two points worth keeping:

- **A `fetch failed` at upload is retryable and leaves nothing half-done.** The
  Worker is replaced atomically or not at all, so a failed upload means the
  previous build kept serving.
- **The migrations were applied once, before the first attempt** — so between the
  apply and the successful upload, production ran the `0.8.0` Worker against the
  `0107` schema. For these three files that was benign, and by design: `0105` only
  removes a capability nothing legitimate used, and `0106`/`0107` are constraints
  derived from what the app already did. It is worth naming because the
  apply-before-deploy rule creates that window on every release, and the size of
  the window is however long the deploy takes to succeed — here, three attempts'
  worth.
