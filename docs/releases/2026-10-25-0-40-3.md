## `0259` — the last role-based rules leave the database, and nothing was lost

`0259` is the database half of removing hard-coded security; the app half was #920. Eleven functions
read columns on `roles` (`scope_residents`, `scope_clinical`, `scope_contacts`, `scope_photos`,
`opens_app`), and **108 row policies and 13 views called them**, so a role column was still deciding
what a login could read even after `0257` made `role_permissions` exactly the union of a role's cards.
Each is now a constant, so none reads a role.

**It was put to Lutan before the cut, separately from the mail question**, because it changes access
quietly — the same treatment `0257` got on `0.40.2`. What needed his eye was not a loss but a **gain**:
measured on dev by impersonating one real login per role through RLS, the result was **33 widenings
across five roles, 103 surfaces unchanged, no losses, and Admin unchanged on every surface.** Six views
have no card gate at all (`current_placement`, `translation_queue`, `immunization_compliance`,
`immunization_duplicate_check`, `resident_current_state`, `app_users`), so every login in the app now
reads them in full. **No card was created to close that**, on his instruction of 2026-10-25 — *"No
creating cards just remove permissions"* — and each is held for Card review 2. He was told this in
plain terms and chose to go ahead.
