**What shipped.** Two notes, both about cards. Settings → Who does what is now opened by a card of its
own (`0262`): Management is given it in the same change so nothing changes for them, any other role
can be given it, and a role without it no longer sees the page. And the Recurring jobs "Assigned to"
list now offers everyone who can sign in, doctors included, instead of leaving them out by role name.
The rest of the release is invisible to a user and is **the end of the security rebuild**: `0263` puts
a card on every view and door that only the front door guarded, and `0264` deletes the inert
leftovers — 60 dead policies, 7 stub functions and 5 retired `roles` columns.

**This release mailed nobody, by decision and by construction.** `major: false`. Lutan was asked
whether it should email him, with the words "major" and "minor" deliberately absent from the question
(`docs/release-procedure.md` step 4). One round trip, and the ambiguity did not recur — the third
release running where that rule has held. **Both deploys confirmed it themselves**: each printed
`no major release new to <env> (was 0.40.3), so no email.`

**Two decisions were put to him before the cut, in one round rather than three:** the mail, and
`0263`'s narrowing of what every login may read. The second is required by the runbook's own rule that
a quietly-changing access decision is named separately rather than absorbed. He chose to go ahead, and
to ship the five unsigned test plans as a stated gap.

## What the before-picture found, and what it turned out to mean

```
role-cards, BEFORE the apply
   doctor: view_residents
```

**The Doctor role holds one card where `0.40.3` recorded five.** `0.40.3`'s record measured
`doctor: 7 cells, 5 cards` both before and after its apply; it now holds `view_residents` alone, and
`doctor-cells` reports **MISSING 12 of 13** against that record's 9 of 13. **Nothing in this release
does that** — `0262` adds a card to Management, `0263` changes view gates and `0264` drops things that
already answered false — and the figure is **identical before and after today's apply**, which is the
measurement that proves it.

**It is not a fault at all. Lutan said, the day this release shipped, that he is using Doctor as his
test role** — holding one card at a time to see what each one actually provides. The changing count
is his work in progress, seen from outside.

**Two things follow, and they matter more than the measurement.** First: **do not restore the
Doctor's cards.** Putting the 13 cells back would destroy whatever he is part-way through measuring,
and the "remedy" this release first wrote down — the new doctor cards — would have done exactly that.
Second: **`doctor-cells` will be red on every release while this continues**, and that red means the
shelter is using the system rather than that something is broken. Whether the check should keep
asserting `0230`'s 13 cells against a role in active use is **his call, not a release manager's**, and
nothing was changed here.

**The honest lesson is about the write-up, not the check.** The before-picture did its job — it
proved this release did not cause the change. What it could not do was say *why*, and this record's
first draft filled that gap with "a route nobody has identified" and "find out how it is changing".
That was a reasonable-sounding inference and it was wrong, and it would have sent the next session to
undo a person's deliberate work. **One question to Lutan settled it in a sentence.** Measure, report
the number, and ask — do not narrate a cause.

## `0263` — every view and door now needs a card

`docs/test-plans/doors-need-a-card.md` with one login per role reading through the database as

**The caveat stated when it was put to him still stands**: that matrix is dev's single-card test

## What broke, if anything

**Nothing broke, and this release has no fault of its own.** Two nuisances, neither worth a change:

- **The cut script was written from scratch again, and met the CRLF hazard again.**
  `src/lib/releases.ts` is CRLF; the first cut attempt failed `markers not found` and was fixed by
  detecting the file's own line ending. It **failed before writing anything**, which is the safe
  direction. `package.json` was done with a one-line string replacement as before. This is the third
  release to pay for it; the standing ask for `scripts/cut-release.mjs` with the cut assertions folded
  in is still a one-liner's worth of value and still unbuilt.
- **`tee` under PowerShell wrote the production deploy log as UTF-16.** `.local/deploy-0.40.4.log`
  needed decoding before it could be grepped. Harmless, and `tee` is still the right choice because it
  passes the exit status through — but **the background task's "exit code 0" was `tail`'s status, not
  the deploy's**, so the deploy was confirmed from `wrangler deployments list` instead. That is the
  `0.40.3` lesson applied: a failed deploy also finishes.
