## `0257` — the access rebuild. It worked, and it cost the Doctor nine cells

`0257_card_grants.sql` fixes a fault that was **live on production**: a card's bundle lived only in
`cards.ts`, and `role_permissions` was recomputed only when someone saved a role on Who does what
with the app handing the bundle in. Any other route to giving a card — `0251` inserted straight into
`role_cards` — granted no access at all. Its own header names the symptom: the Volunteer held 3
cards and 3 of the 4 cells My tasks needs, so My tasks did not work. Dev covered every role, so it
never reproduced there.

The migration recomputes every role as the union of its cards' grants, in the database, by trigger.
Its header states the cost plainly, and it is quoted rather than paraphrased because softening it
would be the whole error:

> Every role is recomputed in this file, and a NOTICE per role lists what was added and what was
> removed (activity names only). Access that came from no card goes away. That is the ruling, and
> the list is the record of what to give back.

**It was put to Lutan separately from the mail question, in the same round**, because it is the one
change in this release that fails quietly: nobody gets an error, a button is simply no longer there,
and he would not have seen it in the release notes. His ruling of 2026-10-24 already authorised the
loss (*"just apply what is required to each card, if someone loses some access I will fix that
later"*), so this was not re-opening a settled decision — it was telling him it happens on **this**
release. He answered go ahead.

### What it achieved, read rather than assumed

```
role-cards-coverage — For each role: which held card needs access the role does not have, and which activity at which level is missing? (After the card-grants release this must print nothing.)
   Every held card is covered by its role's access.

cards-union — For each role: are its permission cells exactly the union of its cards' grants? (Lists cells no card backs, and backed access the role lacks.) role_extras no longer counts (0257).
   doctor: exactly the union of its cards
   head_of_maintenance: exactly the union of its cards
   head_of_medical: exactly the union of its cards
   management: exactly the union of its cards
   second_in_command: exactly the union of its cards
   staff: exactly the union of its cards
   volunteer: exactly the union of its cards
```

**`role-cards-coverage` printing nothing is a milestone, not a routine green.** `CLAUDE.md` says of
it: *"does any role hold a card that does nothing? The answer must always be none"*, and records that
**it did not pass as of 2026-10-24**. It passes now. Every role is exactly the set of cards it holds,
which is the rule the whole security rebuild exists to reach.

### What it cost, and this is the part to act on

```
doctor-cells — Does the doctor role hold the 13 cells migration 0230 gave it, at that level or higher?
   MISSING 9 of 13: medical.blood_tests, medical.diet, medical.immunizations, medical.prescriptions, medical.procedures, medical.visits, reference.add_while_recording, resident.adoption_news, resident.microchip
```

The Doctor held **13 cells, 5 cards, 9 extras** before (read from `0.40.1`'s record) and holds
**7 cells, 5 cards, 9 extras** after. **In plain terms: the vet cannot open or record medical records
on production** — clinic visits, procedures, blood tests, prescriptions, vaccinations and diet, plus
microchip and adoption news, plus one setting.

**The per-role `NOTICE` list the migration promises did not reach this record, and that is a real
gap in the method rather than a detail.** `apply-migrations.mjs` goes through the Supabase Management
API, which returns statement results and not `RAISE NOTICE` output, so the add/remove list the header
describes as *"the record of what to give back"* was not capturable this way. The loss was
established from the other two directions instead — `doctor-cells` names all nine by activity, and
`cards-union` proves no other role lost anything that is not now backed by a card — **but only the
Doctor's loss is itemised here, because the before-picture captured before the apply was
`role-cards` and not `role-cells`.** Cards did not change; cells did. Running `role-cells` **before**
the apply is the one thing the next release of this kind should do differently, and it costs seconds.

**What is being done about it.** Lutan, asked at the moment the loss was established and offered a
pause before the Worker deploy, chose to carry on and fix it after. His remedy, 2026-10-25: *"I will
be creating a new set of doctor cards."* **That is the correct answer and not the obvious one.** The
existing `medical_records` card would restore six of the nine, and `residents` the next two — but
`residents` also grants `resident.record` at edit, `resident.register`, `placement.move`,
`placement.hospital`, `placement.rehome` and `placement.death`, i.e. moving, hospitalising, rehoming
and recording a death. Handing a vet that bundle to recover two cells is the wrong trade. And the
ninth, `reference.add_while_recording`, is granted by **no card at all** — it is in
`src/lib/permissions/fixed-for-now.ts`, one of the hard-coded mechanisms being retired — so
`CLAUDE.md`'s rule applies exactly as written: *"No card for the activity? Create one."* Filed on the
backlog branch, in **Security**, with the verification named (`doctor-cells` back to 13 of 13,
`cards-union` still exact, `role-cards-coverage` still empty).

**This re-opens something the last two editions recorded as closed.** `0.40.1`'s record closed #900's
`pending:` item by measuring that all 13 of `0230`'s doctor cells were live on production, and the
`0.40.1` handover declared its standing list of unreadable production facts **empty** on the strength
of it. `0257` has now removed 9 of those 13 **by design**. The measurement was correct when it was
taken; it is simply no longer true. Recorded here rather than left standing, because a closed item
that has quietly re-opened is worse than one that was never closed.

**Admin was not touched, as required.** `0257` skips the fixed roles. `CLAUDE.md`'s rule is that
Admin sits outside the card system and holds no cards, so a recompute including Admin would have
removed everything and locked the only administrator out of Settings → Who does what — the screen
needed to put any card back, including the Doctor's. Confirmed by the role list: Admin does not
appear in `role-cells` or `role-cards` output at all.
