### What is in it

The printable manual with its screenshots back; delete and remove confirmations
that name what will be lost, with Cancel focused, plus taller buttons and an
offline warning bar; a clearer message when a session has timed out; and a note
on Management → Stock between counts when a figure was typed by hand during the
dates being compared.

### What is in it that nobody was told about, deliberately

Two things in this release have no release note, and that was the right call
rather than an oversight:

- **#268 bumped Next to `16.3.8`, clearing a Critical RCE advisory**
  (GHSA-vcvr-r3jv-pc5j, `next/og` ImageResponse). `npm audit --omit=dev
  --audit-level=high` reported one critical vulnerability on `0.12.0` and
  **`found 0 vulnerabilities`** on this release.
- **#266 added the audit-log table** that `0.13.0`'s Recent changes page reads.

Neither is something a shelter user would notice, so neither earned a note. That
is also precisely why this release is `major: false`: release mail is built from
the notes, so a major would have mailed four lines about a PDF and a dialog while
saying nothing about the security fix. Minor was right for that reason as much as
the usual one.

them at all. The distinction matters because applying migrations moves no code:
after that apply, the schema was ahead of the app on both copies, which is the
safe direction and the one the ordering rule wants.

### The date, and the window that keeps coming up

The register entry reads `2026-10-02` although the cut was made on `2026-10-01`:
the clock rolled over between the cut and the merge, and the entry was moved to
today on the precedent Lutan set for `0.10.0`. At the moment of that change
`date +%Y-%m-%d` gave `2026-10-02` while `date -u` still gave `2026-10-01`, so
the two clocks disagreed by a day and the entry follows Thailand's, per release
`0.1.0`'s first note. **Fifth time that midnight-to-07:00 window has mattered
here, and the second time it caught the release tooling rather than a feature.**
