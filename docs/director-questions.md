# Questions for the Director and the accountant: Chiang Mai, November 2026

Written 2026-11-13. This is the order to ask them in, one question per line, each with what the answer unlocks. It builds on the 10 November list and replaces it for use in the room: everything is on one page so two conversations become one. Roles only, no names (this repo is public). The copy Lutan uses has a blank after every question for the answer.

Settled already, so do not reopen: the shop is built into our own website; it is mostly drop-shipped with one combined order per supplier; about 50 orders a month to start; Thailand and overseas; the donation QR is a plain PromptPay code matched by hand.

## A. With the Director and the accountant together (ask first; one fact)

1. Is the foundation formally announced as a public charitable organisation? *Yes or no. A no closes `#99zuk` (no schema, no privacy decision, the ordinary receipt is unaffected). A yes opens questions 2 and 3.*
2. If yes: will the accountant enter gifts in e-Donation herself, or should the app prepare them for her? *Decides whether we build a list and export or only a tick box.*
3. If yes: how long must a donor's 13-digit ID be kept? *This is sensitive data under the Thai privacy law, so the answer sets the retention rule before any schema.*
4. Does money from the shop count as trading income for the foundation, and does that change its status or its tax? *Decides whether the shop needs any accounting set-up before launch.* (`#cjxq0`)
5. Is anything else tax-shaped waiting, such as the fuller financial report? *Unlocks `#00e3u`.*

## B. With the Director

6. Who will put adoptable animals on the website, a few a week? *She names a person and the work starts that day. Nothing is built. Target: 30 public within a month, then each new adoptable animal within a week of arriving.* (`#5j6kj`)
7. When she said drop-shipment and B2B, did she mean selling to businesses (vets, pet shops, cafés, Shelter Friends), businesses selling through our site with a cut to the shelter, or neither? *Neither closes `#hj1c5` at once, because the shop covers it. One of the first two gets an options paper.* (`#hj1c5`)
8. Only if she chose one of those two: what would be sold and to whom; has any partner business been spoken to; who handles business invoices and tax receipts; how many orders a month? *Decides whether it is worth any effort.*

## C. The shop (her questionnaire; `#cjxq0`)

9. What are the first products, and the price of each? *The first catalogue.*
10. What margin does she want on each? *Sets selling price from supplier cost.*
11. Has she spoken to any printer or wholesaler? Names, minimum orders, quoted costs. *Without a supplier there is nothing to connect to; with one we know whether it takes orders by email, file or its own system.*
12. What does postage cost in Thailand and abroad, and does an overseas buyer pay in baht or their own money? *Checkout shipping prices and the payment choice.*
13. What happens on a return or a wrong size? *The returns wording and staff steps.*
14. Which person answers "where is my order"? A person, not a department. *Who gets the order emails and the staff screen.*
15. Will the shelter hold any stock itself, and who packs and posts it? *Whether the shop needs stock counts and a staff packing list.*
16. Does she have the logo files in print quality? *Suppliers cannot start without them.*

## D. The phone day (`#s0jgi`)

17. Which shelter worker and which volunteer can follow the manual on a phone for one day, and on which day? *Lets the manual review happen; the Thai manual waits on it.* Take the one-page sheet with you if the planner has one by then: for each step, note what did not match, what was missing and any word the shelter does not use.

## What to bring home

Answers 1 to 3 and 6 and 7 settle whole items. Items 9 to 16 turn the shop into a written scope. Record the answers on the items when Lutan is back.
