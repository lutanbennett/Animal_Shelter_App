# Public website review — requested 2026-10-13, written 2026-10-13

A review of the public pages of Lanna Care for Animals as a first-time visitor would meet
them: on a phone (375 px wide) and on a laptop (1280 px), in English and in Thai, on a local
dev server running this branch against the **dev** database. Production was not opened. Nothing
was changed: this review is a document, and every suggestion in it is written as a backlog
item for Lutan to accept or leave.

This repository is public. Every screenshot shows dev data only, and the report names no
donor, adopter, carer or private person. Where a suggestion needs the shelter's own words,
a draft is given in English for the Director to correct; nothing drafted here is published.

It builds on the redesign that shipped on 2026-09-26 (`docs/decisions/2026-09-26-public-site-redesign-part-1…3`),
the 2026-10-04 findings pass, and the open public-site items in `docs/backlog.md`, and says
where it agrees with them rather than proposing them again.
